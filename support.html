Help & Support

Updated September 29, 2026

Get in touch

Email meal-planner@meloryn.app. Include your device type, app version, and a description of the problem. For purchase problems, include the store and order reference if available; never send full card details.

Restore a purchase

Use Restore Purchases in Settings → Subscription or on the purchase screen. Use the same Apple or Google store account used for the purchase. Contact support if access is still missing.

Cancel a subscription

Manage or cancel your subscription through Apple or Google Play. Deleting the app or its data does not cancel a store subscription.

Delete your account and data

In the app, open Settings → Delete Account and confirm. This deletes your anonymous account, profile, saved recipe references, meal plans, pantry, shopping data, and associated local app data after the server confirms deletion. A failed operation can be retried. For help with deletion, email us; we may need information to identify and verify your anonymous account. Store and payment-provider records are handled separately.

Recipes and shopping quantities

Check recipe instructions, ingredient labels, and quantities before cooking. Compatible quantities can be combined. When a quantity cannot be reliably combined, the shopping list can show the ingredient name; open it to inspect the quantities in each recipe. Pantry entries are maintained by you, not automatically depleted.

Language and preferences

Change your language and food preferences in Settings. Interface language and recipe language can differ. Dietary filters cannot guarantee that a recipe is safe for an allergy.
