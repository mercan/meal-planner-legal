MEAL PLANNER

Privacy Policy

Updated September 29, 2026

Who this policy covers

This policy describes how the operator of Meal Planner handles information in the app and this website. Contact: meal-planner@meloryn.app.

Information you provide

Meal Planner uses an anonymous account identifier rather than an email/password login. It stores your entered name, age range, language, cooking and meal preferences, serving counts, meal times, dietary choices, allergies, disliked ingredients, saved recipe references, meal plans, pantry items, and shopping-list changes. Dietary and allergy information may be sensitive. Provide only information you want used for your plan.

How information is used

We use this information to operate your account, save and synchronize your choices, show recipes, maintain plans and shopping lists, verify subscription access, and respond to support requests. Network services also process connection information such as IP addresses. Account credentials are stored using the device’s secure storage; app data is also stored locally and on our backend.

Service providers

Our hosting and database services process app data to operate the service. FatSecret receives recipe searches and filters through our backend; loading third-party recipe images also makes requests to image hosts. RevenueCat processes the anonymous app identifier and subscription and purchase information to verify access. Apple or Google processes payments; we do not receive your full payment-card details. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy, FatSecret’s privacy policy, and your store’s privacy information.

Permissions and tracking

The app can request notification permission for meal-reminder preferences. You can change permission in your device settings. This version does not include an advertising or product-analytics SDK. This static website has no analytics scripts or cookies added by us; GitHub Pages may process technical connection data under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Retention and deletion

Account data is retained while your account is active or until you delete it. Use Settings → Delete Account to remove our account, profile, plan, pantry, grocery, saved-recipe and membership records and the app’s associated local data. A short-lived deletion receipt is retained for up to seven days to let deletion finish safely; billing-event deduplication records expire after 30 days. Recipe response caching is temporary, not a permanent recipe archive. Support correspondence may be retained to resolve your request and meet applicable obligations. Store and RevenueCat transaction records may remain under their own retention rules; app deletion does not cancel subscriptions.

Your choices and rights

You can edit preferences and delete your account in the app. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing, receive a copy of data, or complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us to exercise these rights; identity verification may be required. Where applicable, processing is based on providing the service, your consent for optional permissions or sensitive information, and legitimate interests in security and support. Service providers may process data outside your country under applicable safeguards.

Children and security

Meal Planner is not specifically designed for children. Minors should use the service with the consent required by local law. Contact us if you believe a child provided information without appropriate consent. We use access controls and secure credential storage, but no system can promise absolute security.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. The date above identifies this version. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law.

Shared homes

If you create or join a shared home, your chosen display name, meal plan, shopping list and pantry entries are visible to the other members. Personal dietary preferences are not automatically shared. Invitations expire after 24 hours and can be used once. Leaving or removal ends access; the app clears the shared plan when it reconnects and restores your previous personal plan. Closing the home or deleting the owner’s account deletes the shared home for all members. Information copied by another member outside the app cannot be remotely erased.
